f1nanc3/privacy

privacy

last updated 22 november 2026

f1nanc3 is a personal finance app run by one person for their own use, and for a few people they invite. There's no sign-up: accounts are created by the owner. It shows where your money goes, using your own bank transactions.

what it keeps

  • your sign-in: an email address and a password (kept hashed by Supabase, never readable)
  • your bank transactions: date, amount, the bank's description, the payment type, the balance after it, and the last 4 digits of the account number. Full account numbers and sort codes are never stored.
  • the categories you choose, and which merchants they apply to
  • if you connect a bank: which bank, which accounts (last 4 digits), when the bank's approval ends, and when transactions were last fetched

where it comes from

  • statements you upload (read on your device first; nothing is sent until you choose to import)
  • if you connect a bank, from your bank through Enable Banking, a regulated account information service provider. You approve this at your bank and can withdraw it at any time there or with "disconnect" in the app. f1nanc3 can only read transactions; it can't make payments or move money.

where it's kept and who can see it

  • in a database at Supabase, hosted in the EU (Frankfurt)
  • each account can only ever read its own data; the database enforces this
  • nothing is sold or shared, and there are no adverts, trackers or analytics
  • on your device, the app keeps only your sign-in and your day/night choice (in the browser's storage); your transactions are not stored on the device

how long

Transactions are kept for the current month and the 12 before it; older ones are deleted automatically every night. Disconnecting a bank stops new fetches but keeps what's already there.

your choices

You can disconnect your bank at any time, and ask for your account and all of its data to be deleted. To ask, contact the owner through the project on GitHub.
