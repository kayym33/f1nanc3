# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for Claude Code in this repository.

## Overview

f1nanc3 is a personal finance web app (PWA) at https://f1nanc3.kaylamc.uk: bank statements in, spending by category out. It's a sibling of t1ld3 (`~/Projects/resonance`, whose `phone/` app this one is modelled on) and shares its Supabase project (`zuzpyrwoovdavbuqrqpx`) and accounts, but nothing else. Plain JS modules, no build step, no dependencies; GitHub Pages deploys `main` (`.github/workflows/pages.yml`).

The owner wants nothing they won't use: **ask before adding any feature** that isn't in the plan below.

## Architecture

- `js/app.js`: hash routing (`#/`, `#/m/2026-11`, `#/tx?month=&category=`, `#/tx/<id>`, `#/import`, `#/me`), header trail, tab bar, and dispatch: elements carry `data-action`/`data-form`/`data-input` names that each view exports in `actions`/`forms`/`inputs`. Views return `{crumbs, html}`; everything from a statement goes through `esc()`.
- `js/cloud.js`: auth (session in `localStorage` under `f1nanc3.session`) and PostgREST calls. `js/config.js` holds the public URL and publishable key; `?api=` overrides it on localhost only.
- `js/data.js` (`Finance`): loads the window's transactions and the rules into memory. Bank data is never cached on the device (not in localStorage, not in the service worker). On every load, `reread()` re-runs `merchantOf`/`guessCategory` over what's stored and patches anything that now reads differently (keeping `manual` categories and server-paired transfers), so improving the reader fixes old imports too; re-importing wouldn't, since duplicates are skipped.
- Real RBS descriptions (seen 2026-11-22): card lines `4378 20NOV26 CD , MERCHANT 123 , TOWN GB` (the suffix can be CD, C, D or nothing), online payments `To A/C 12345678 , NAME , Via Mobile Xfer` / `From A/C ...` (merchant `to a/c …5678`), round-ups `ROUND UP TO/FROM 12345678` (type TFR, filed as transfers), interest type INT (`interest`).
- No-UI logic: `js/csv.js` (finds columns by name; RBS layout is Date, Type, Description, Value, Balance, Account Name, Account Number, with `'`-prefixed fields; ids are a hash of account|date|amount|description|occurrence so re-imports are no-ops), `js/categories.js` (`merchantOf` makes the key rules are stored under, `guessCategory`), `js/money.js` (pounds, months, the window), `js/summary.js`.
- Database (`supabase/schema.sql`, safe to re-run): `fin_transactions` (PK `user_id, id`; `category_source` auto/rule/manual; only auto/rule get re-guessed), `fin_rules` (merchant → category), `fin_mark_transfers()` (security invoker; pairs equal and opposite amounts across accounts within a day), and the `f1nanc3-retention` cron job (keeps the current month plus 12).
- The river (`#/river`, `#/river/<month>`): `js/flow.js` is the no-UI shape (`riverBands`, `allocateRows`, `riverLayout`: a trunk about half the height fanning out to slots; categories in the fixed `CATEGORIES` order so months morph rather than reorder) and `js/views/river.js` draws it on one canvas of monospace cells, flowing at 10fps and easing between months (the last shape is kept in module state). Views can return `mounted()`, which `app.js` calls once the HTML is on the page; the river stops itself when its canvas leaves the page. Reduced motion draws it still.
- Wrapped (`#/wrapped`, from a link on the overview): `js/wrapped.js` computes the last 365 days' stats with no UI (`yearStats`, `longestWithout`, `regularPayments`, the start of subscription detection) and `js/views/wrapped.js` is a full-screen deck (`body.wrapped-open` hides the tabs) with count-up numbers, glyph pictures whose rows draw in, auto-advance (paused while held), arrows, swipes and Esc. A card is left out when the data doesn't support it.
- Bank connection (stage 2): `supabase/functions/bank` (POST as the signed-in user: `start` → Enable Banking `/auth` url, `finish` with the redirect's `code`/`state` → `/sessions`, saves `fin_bank_links`, fetches history at once since banks only give full history shortly after approval; `sync`; `disconnect`) and `bank-sync` (cron at 05:00 and 06:00 UTC, acts only in the UK's 6 o'clock hour; `{"force": true}` overrides; checks `CRON_SECRET`). Shared code: `_shared/enablebanking.ts` (RS256 JWT from `ENABLE_BANKING_APP_ID`/`ENABLE_BANKING_KEY`, PKCS#1 or #8 PEM; `psuHeaders` lift the bank's ~4/day background limit for fetches you start) and `_shared/sync.ts`; both import `js/bank.js` (`bankRow`: booked GBP transactions only, id `eb` + hash of account and `entry_reference`; `notAlreadyThere`: CSV and bank rows cancel out by account, date and amount, both ways). `fin_bank_links` is readable by its owner (some columns only) and written only by the functions; `fin_mark_transfers_for(uuid)` is the service-role version of the pairing. The app comes back to `/?code=&state=` and `me.finishConnecting` handles it; `me.bankBanner` shows the 7-day expiry banner on every page.
- Subs (`#/subs`, `#/subs/<merchant, URI-encoded>`): `js/subscriptions.js` (`findSubscriptions`: outgoing, not transfers/income/cash, ≥3 months, ≤1.5 payments a month, median gap 24–38 days, consecutive amounts within 35%; `ended` after 45 days; `priceChange` over 2%) and `js/services.js` (`SERVICES`: name, kind subscription|bill, match keywords like the category ones, account/cancel URL). Your choices live in `fin_subscriptions` (merchant → kind, url, hidden) and win over the list. `views/subs.js` draws the list and a payment's page (`js/chart.js` columns). Links inside a tappable row are plain links: `app.js` skips `data-action` handling for an `<a href>` without one.
- Charts (`#/charts`): `js/trends.js` (no UI: `inOut`, `categoryGrid`, `pace`, `gridCsv`, `niceCeiling`) and `views/charts.js`, hand-written SVG drawn in `mounted()` at the measured width and style (redrawn by a ResizeObserver), one shared `.chart-tip`. Each chart is a `spec` (series, labels, `tip(i)`, `range(a, b)` for a dragged stretch) drawn by `svg()` and wired by `interact()`: hover/tap shows a point, dragging highlights a stretch and fills the section's `[data-readout]`, a tap or Esc clears. Three styles (`js/chartstyle.js`, settings, per device, `<html data-charts>`): terminal (default; area lines, right-hand axis, price tags, crosshair tag, the current month dashed), terminal-rg, soft (rounded bars, cards). Colours are CSS variables (`--series-in/out/last`, `--good`, `--bad`) per style and theme; every pair passed the dataviz skill's validator (green/red needed #0A5C45/#D84A2C by day and #5CCB8F/#F0574A at night to clear deuteranopia). Categories use small multiples, never a palette of hues. SVG ignores `hidden`: toggle `visibility`. Bill days (toggle on the pace chart, `f1nanc3.billDays` per device, on by default): `subscriptions.js` `billDays` puts each live subscription/bill on its day this month (paid = faint, still to come = bold), `projection` steps the month's total up on each one still to come (a dashed line, and "about £X by the month's end"); labels are stacked into three rows by measured width so they never overlap.
- Pay and balances: `js/income.js` (`regularIncome`: income from the same source about monthly, allowing pay to vary 60% step to step, typical amount = middle of the last three; `expectedIncome`: not yet in this month and due by its end) feeds the overview's "+ pay ~£X due the 26th" / "about +£Y after pay" and the charts' still-to-come list (pay never enters the spending line). `js/balances.js` `latestBalances` takes each account's last transaction balance, finding the day's last by following the balance chain (stored order is unreliable). Accounts are named and marked spending/savings in settings (`fin_accounts`); `finance.accountName()` is used in the list and the overview's balance ("to spend" vs "saved", as of the last import).
- Navigation: tabs overview · list · river · subs · charts; settings (the old me page, with import under it) is the ⚙ at the top right (`#/settings`; `#/me` still works).
- The bank connection is parked: `BANK_CONNECTION = false` in `js/config.js` hides its settings section and banner. Enable Banking's restricted (own accounts) mode lists only EU/EEA countries, not the UK. The server side (functions, `fin_bank_links`, the `f1nanc3-bank-sync` cron) is deployed but idle.
- Look: copied from t1ld3's `phone/style.css`; day/night follow the device; the accent is fixed orange (no picker, by the owner's choice).
- New files must be added to `SHELL` in `sw.js`.

Push needs care: t1ld3's `push_subscriptions` table and functions send to every subscription a user has, so f1nanc3's subscriptions must be kept apart (for example an `app` column) or t1ld3's reminders would arrive here too.
