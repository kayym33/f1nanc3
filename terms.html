f1nanc3/terms

terms

last updated 22 november 2026

  • f1nanc3 is a personal project, used by its owner and a few people they invite. It isn't a commercial service and isn't for sale.
  • It reads your transactions to show where your money goes. It can't make payments or move money, and it never asks for your bank login: you approve access at your bank.
  • Categories and totals are worked out automatically and can be wrong. Nothing in it is financial advice; check anything important against your bank.
  • It's provided as it is, without any guarantee that it will always work or be available, and the owner isn't responsible for losses from using it.
  • Your data is handled as the privacy page says.
  • Access can end at any time: you can disconnect your bank or ask for your account to be deleted, and the owner can close the app.
